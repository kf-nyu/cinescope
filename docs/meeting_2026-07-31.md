## Storage (local run — machine-specific; not for public paths)

Use your own `CINESCOPE_DATA_ROOT` from `.env`. Example patterns:

- Home directory: `$HOME/cinescope-data`

## Design choices

- Principals explode row counts → aggregate to one row per film
- Roles kept: actor, actress, director, writer, producer, composer, cinematographer, editor
- Reputation priors: `start_year` strictly before current film
- Highly rated prior: `average_rating >= 7.0`
- Known person rule: ≥10 career movies, ≥100k votes, ≥7.0 mean rating
- Oscar outcomes are post-release — exclude from pre-release hit features
